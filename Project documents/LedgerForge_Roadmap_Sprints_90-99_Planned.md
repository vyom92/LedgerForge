### Sprint 98 — Investment / ISP Holdings and External Valuation Foundation

Queue: `FW-P3-20`, `FW-P3-21`, `FW-P3-22`, `FW-P3-23`, `FW-P3-29`, with only bounded external-price/NAV retrieval under `FW-P3-27` and manual fallback under `FW-P3-28` where genuinely required.

This is a **planning position, not permission to create an investment megasprint**. Entry discovery must first establish whether mutual-fund holdings, brokerage/identity foundations, Qatar Airways ISP holdings and their external valuation adapters form one coherent architecture/acceptance boundary. If they do not, split the outcome and update this prepared roadmap before implementation rather than widening Sprint 98.

Holding ownership, units, contributions/cost evidence and identity must exist before external price/NAV evidence can produce a portfolio value. The ordinary valuation boundary is `owned units × native-currency price/NAV = derived native market value`; external prices never establish owned units.

For Qatar Airways ISP, `FW-P3-29` owns policy/holding/contribution semantics. Prospective Zurich lookup uses exact Fund Code + expected USD for `N0USD`, `USDL3`, `3UUSD` and `B0280`; fund name is secondary. Read-only discovery has technically replayed the productive public FE request, but provider price date remains `PRICE_DATE_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, Change-% period remains unestablished, and automated persistent-cache rights remain an implementation gate. The historical temporary `0.75` workbook multiplier is explicitly excluded from Sprint 98 and future ordinary `B0280` valuation.

For Indian mutual funds, `FW-P3-20` owns the holding/domain model and **Direct-plan-only** identity. Recurring lookup uses exact configured AMFI scheme code + ISIN + plan/option metadata; Regular plans never substitute. `FW-P3-27` may use the technically qualified current AMFI `NAVAll.txt` publication after provider/cache qualification; expected INR is configured/qualified separately because the feed does not itself carry an explicit currency field. `FW-P3-28` remains manual/custom fallback, not the primary Indian-mutual-fund domain.

### Sprint 99 — General FX and Reporting-Currency Foundation

Queue: `FW-P3-30`, `FW-P3-31`, `FW-P3-32`, `FW-P3-33`.

Sprint 99 is **general reporting/valuation FX**, not Salary remittance. Al Dar is explicitly excluded from this sprint and remains a future Salary/This Month/India-remittance direction under `FW-P3-08` plus a future ADR-045 alignment or successor architecture.

The user-selected general conversion route is `INR → QAR → USD`, using a general QAR→INR leg and a general QAR→USD leg. `ExchangeRate-API` at `open.er-api.com/v6/latest/QAR` is a **technically qualified provider candidate**, not a permanent selected vendor, accepted architecture or unique allowed provider. Entry discovery/ADR must approve provider choice, attribution, provider-specific freshness/fallback, storage and derived-value semantics before implementation.

For each accepted reference snapshot preserve both raw source legs and orientation, provider/source timestamp, fetch timestamp separately, provider/source identity, source-response digest and derivation algorithm/version. Native `Money` remains authority; derived conversion never overwrites imported or holding values. Failed/missing reference data remains stale/unavailable rather than zero, and no background polling requirement is implied.
