# Product vision

LedgerForge is the owner's private, single-user, offline-first macOS personal-finance app: a nicer replacement for the Budget Analysis Google sheet. Import the owner's authentic monthly bank, card and investment statements into one truthful durable place, then use that unified data to understand transactions, spending, budgets and current financial position.

The owner should spend less time collecting and reconciling records while retaining explicit control and explainable source provenance. Financial correctness, durable persistence and deterministic native-currency meaning come before speed. An attractive, readable, convenient daily workflow is valuable in its own right; it need not be justified as a financial defect.

The adopted personal direction includes practical R1 Transactions/Dashboard/appearance, verified backup/restore, accepted Salary actuals/history and current This Month planning with bounded current Al Dar/manual override, current holdings/valuation, current market FX/net worth and a small truthful supported-source matrix. Complete structured export is optional. Current holdings do not require invented investment history; current FX does not require historical conversion. These are product outcomes with real evidence/architecture gates, not an implementation order or support claim.

[SCOPE_DECISIONS](SCOPE_DECISIONS.md) owns settled choices, exclusions and reopening conditions. Public/commercial platforms, generalized tooling and hypothetical source expansion do not define this app. Existing useful local diagnostics, supported credentials and financial/recovery safeguards remain. No feature is inferred merely from a module name, artwork or schema capacity.
