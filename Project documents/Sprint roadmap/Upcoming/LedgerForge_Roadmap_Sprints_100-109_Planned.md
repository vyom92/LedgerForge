Retain the accepted foundations: single and batch Import Centre flows, deterministic routing, explicit statement confirmation, provider-owned persistence, canonical hydration, duplicate/equivalence contracts, credentials, Qatar Airways Salary source truth and Salary History, current-month budget/planner semantics, migration integrity and privacy.

Current Al Dar amount-bound QAR→INR evidence serves only Salary/This Month planning, with a manual current-rate override and truthful stale/unavailable behavior. Current net-worth uses separate qualified market FX, including direct INR→USD and QAR→USD evidence. Native Money, exact owned units, price/rate provenance and non-overlapping membership remain authoritative; missing components produce an incomplete result, never zero. These are planned acceptance requirements, not accepted architectures or current implementation claims.
