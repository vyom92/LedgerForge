**Historical discovery conclusion — 2026-09-10:**

The owner retained FW-P0-19 **only for review of three existing Axis-bank CSV/PDF/XLS groups**. No generic future cross-format identity work, automatic suppression or replacement is selected. Engineering parity and durable equivalence remain separate.

The retained 2026-09-10 comparison found three logical Axis-bank groups with three distinct carriers each, covering two opaque account identities. All three groups had nonzero financial/reference projection mismatch; none was an exact cross-format financial/reference match. Explain actual mismatches with independent source projections before proposing any financial relationship. Literal text differences alone do not determine financial equivalence.

Two same-date/opposite-direction/amount shapes existed, but there was no nonempty UPI/narration-digest match, and the independent oracle lacked currency for that comparison. This is **insufficient transfer evidence**, not a confirmed transfer. No new matching/suppression relationship was established.

Four HDFC PDF/XLS pairs had zero financial/reference projection mismatches even though every literal narration differed. These are already governed by ADR-042, not new work. Axis-card evidence comprised 32 carriers/18 logical statements and established no new relationship beyond ADR-044. CBQ lineage remains ADR-043. The scope reset removed hypothetical additional relationship/parity/event/stress cards without weakening existing regression duties.

A transfer proposal must preserve both original transactions and create only an accepted relationship, with exact account/currency/date/reference/direction evidence, ambiguity/multiplicity handling and independently checked financial effects. Same amount/day, apparent narration similarity or balancing totals cannot establish identity. Reconciliation additionally needs genuine period controls, completeness/gap/overlap status and explicit unresolved exceptions. It must not infer whole-account completeness from selected statements.

Historical backfill requires actual supplied date ranges, gaps, overlap and legacy unfingerprinted history, plus the accepted duplicate and family-specific correction contract. It cannot manufacture dates/source order, suppress rows by guessed equivalence or claim completeness. Missing genuine large/zero-activity cases remain untested; fabricated statements are prohibited.
